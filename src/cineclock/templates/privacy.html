Privacy

Short version: cineclock has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It keeps only what it needs to show you showtimes and, if you sign in, your saved films. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Browsing

You don't need an account to use cineclock. The site doesn't set tracking cookies or run analytics. Posters and backdrops load straight from TMDB, so TMDB sees the same request any website image would. Ticket links take you to each theatre's own site, where its own privacy policy applies.

Your Letterboxd username

If you connect Letterboxd, your username is saved in your own browser. To read your public watchlist and diary, the username goes to cineclock's small relay on Cloudflare, which reads those public Letterboxd pages and keeps a copy for up to 6 hours so Letterboxd isn't asked too often. Disconnect removes it from your browser.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. When you sign in, cineclock gets your Google account ID and email address from Google, and nothing else from your Google account. It then stores, with your account:

  • the films and theatres you save,
  • your settings, like which rating source you prefer,
  • your Letterboxd username or IMDb watchlist, if you add them,
  • your alert choices, and which films you've already been emailed about.

This lives in a database on Cloudflare. Your browser keeps a sign-in key (a cookie, and a copy in its own storage) so you stay signed in; signing out or deleting your account ends it. cineclock never sees your Google password.

Emails

cineclock only emails you if you turn on alerts. Every email has a one-tap unsubscribe link, and turning alerts off stops them.

Deleting your data

Deleting your account removes everything listed above, right away. You can also email me and I'll do it for you.
